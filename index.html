Digital Payments & Navigation Apps

Alipay & WeChat Pay

Almost everywhere in China is cashless. Download Alipay or WeChat before arrival and link your international Visa/Mastercard for seamless QR code payments at street stalls, taxis, and high-end restaurants.

DiDi (Ride Hailing)

China's equivalent to Uber. Available in English via the Alipay mini-program or standalone app. Essential for getting around without speaking fluent Mandarin.

SIM / eSIM & VPN Note

Buy a roaming eSIM (like Airalo or Holafly) before you arrive to bypass internet restrictions for Google, WhatsApp, and Instagram. Alternatively, subscribe to a reliable roaming plan.
